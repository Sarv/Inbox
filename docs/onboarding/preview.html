    One Sarv sign-in

    Sarv connects email and AI together. Go straight to the available models without a second sign-in. The user can still choose another AI provider.

    Keep the way forward clear

    One primary action per screen. Preserve details on Back and retry. AI, antivirus and sending can be finished later; incoming mail must connect first.

    Connection comes before activation

    Sarv sign-in connects AI, but processing starts only after model selection and agreement. Other connected AI providers also skip repeated configuration.

    Optional attachment protection

    Scanner access is approved separately from email and AI access. After connection, scan files before viewing or downloading. If setup is skipped, show the existing warning; a configured scanner failure still blocks the file.

    Finish without extra lessons

    Open the inbox after setup. Offer keyboard shortcuts and extensions from the app afterward.
