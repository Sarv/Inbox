What is shared

  • Bytes of attachments you select, with random item IDs and sizes.
  • Message text only with separate setup permission and per-scan consent.
  • Content may contain personal information even without email headers.

What stays on your device

  • Your Gmail / IMAP credentials and account identity.
  • The local mapping between a ticket and its message.
  • Real attachment filenames, subjects and envelope headers.

Temporary processing, bounded retention

  • Proposed content lifetime: scan completion or at most 5 minutes after ticket creation.
  • Proposed result lifetime: at most 15 minutes after the terminal state.
  • No intended persistent content storage, sample sharing, advertising or model training.

Your controls

  • No account selected and no remote sharing enabled by default.
  • Every scan is manual. Body scanning is a separate choice.
  • Disable scanning to stop new uploads and request cancellation. Transmitted bytes cannot be recalled.

The server must demonstrate these promises.

Metadata declares a retention policy; it does not verify deletion. The future service needs cleanup and logging checks before release. Public Gmail uploads also need accurate privacy disclosures and Google's applicable verification / security assessment.

No scanner, real credential, mailbox, network request or persistent storage is connected to this preview.
