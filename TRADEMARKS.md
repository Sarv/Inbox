**Short version:** the code is shared under the [Sarv Community License](./LICENSE);
the Sarv name and logos are not. The licence asks you to *keep* our branding on
copies you pass on — that is the opposite of most projects, and it exists so
users always know where the software came from. It does not let you put our name
on your own work.

Copyright and trademarks are separate things, and this file covers only the
second.

## What you must do

If you redistribute Sarv Inbox — as-is or modified, and remember that
redistribution itself needs to satisfy [LICENSE](./LICENSE) §5 and §6 — you must
leave the branding where you found it: the product name, the icons, the About
screen, the window title, the installer metadata, the copyright notices and the
links back to us. Removing or replacing any of it is rebranding, and rebranding
is not permitted without our written consent.

If you modified the software, you must *add* to that branding a clear statement
that your build is unofficial, that you made the changes, and that Sarv does not
supply, support or endorse it. Somewhere a user will actually see it — the About
screen and your README, not a comment in the source.

## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your project works with, integrates with, is built on, or
  is a modified build of Sarv Inbox — in plain text, not in your logo or product
  name.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, comparisons and academic
  work.
- Use unmodified logo files to illustrate the software you are writing about.
- Keep the marks in place when you redistribute, exactly as the licence requires.

## What you may not do without our written consent

- Use the marks in the name, logo, domain, app-store listing or social handle of
  your own product, service, company or fork.
- Register the marks, or anything confusingly similar, anywhere in the world.
- Present a modified build as an official Sarv release, or imply partnership,
  sponsorship or endorsement.
- Sell merchandise bearing the marks.
- Alter the logos: no recolouring, stretching, cropping, rotating, adding
  effects, or combining them with other marks.

## Getting permission

Write to [legal@sarv.com](mailto:legal@sarv.com) describing what you want to use
and where. For commercial, OEM, hosting or white-label arrangements — the cases
where you probably *do* want to change the branding — write to
[licensing@sarv.com](mailto:licensing@sarv.com); a commercial licence can grant
branding rights this policy withholds.

We would rather talk than send a lawyer. If you think you may be over the line,
ask first.
