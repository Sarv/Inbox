MORE DETAILS

What Sarv Inbox does

Sarv Inbox is a desktop email client for macOS, Windows and Linux. Connect a Gmail or Google Workspace account, Sarv Mail or any other IMAP mailbox, then read, search, write, reply to, forward, organise and delete your mail in one app.

  • Conversations as threads or as a chat view, with fast search on your computer.
  • Labels and folders, drafts, attachments, follow-up reminders and snoozing.
  • Spam and phishing protection that checks sender authentication and blocklists.
  • Optional AI help once you connect an AI provider (Sarv AI or your own): sorting mail into categories, suggested reply drafts and AI search.

How Sarv Inbox uses your Google data

When you connect a Gmail or Google Workspace account, Sarv Inbox asks Google for access to your mail (https://mail.google.com/) and to your name and email address. It uses that access only to provide the features above: downloading, showing, sending, organising and deleting your mail over IMAP and SMTP, and managing the app's labels. Your mail is stored on your computer in an encrypted database, and Sarv runs no server that keeps a copy. Parts of messages go to an AI provider only after you connect one.

Sarv Inbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The privacy policy has the details.
